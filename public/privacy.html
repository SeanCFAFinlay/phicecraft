Privacy policy

Last updated 24 November 2026

PHormation is built so your plays stay with you. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Everything you make in PHormation — plays, drills, practice plans, rosters, player names, team names and logos — is saved in your browser's storage on the device you are using. It is not sent to us. If you clear your browser's data or uninstall the app, it is deleted; use Export all plays in the menu to keep a backup.

Sharing a play

When you share a play by link, the play itself is written into the link, after the #. Browsers do not send that part of a link to a web server, so the play is not stored on our servers. Anyone who has the link can open the play, so share it only with people you want to see it. Videos and images you export are made on your device.

Crash reports

If the app crashes, it sends a short technical report so the problem can be fixed. A report contains the error message, the part of the program where it happened, the version of the app, your browser type, your screen size and the time. It never contains your plays, player names, rosters or anything you typed, and it never contains the shared play in a link. Reports are kept in our hosting provider's logs for their standard retention period and are used only to fix bugs.

Hosting

PHormation is hosted by Vercel. Like any website, their servers process your IP address and basic request details to deliver the app, under Vercel's privacy policy. We do not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.

Children and youth players

PHormation is a tool for coaches. Rosters you enter stay on your device, but we recommend using first names or jersey numbers for youth players, especially in plays you share.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
